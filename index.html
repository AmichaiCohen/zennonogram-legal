How to Play

Each row and column displays clue numbers indicating the lengths of consecutive filled blocks in that line (in order, separated by at least one empty space).

Use Fill mode to mark solid blocks and Cross (×) mode to rule out empty cells. Drag smoothly along any row or column to paint multiple cells in one stroke.

Grid Sizes & Daily Puzzle

Choose from 5×5 (Easy), 10×10 (Medium), 15×15 (Hard), or the shared Daily 10×10 challenge. Every puzzle is verified by a line-based logical solver to be uniquely solvable without guessing.

Smart Hints, Undo & Offline Play

Tap Undo to revert your last tap or drag stroke. Tap Hint to reveal one logically deducible cell. All progress, daily streaks, and settings are saved automatically on your device so you can play 100% offline.

Remove Ads & Restore Purchases

ZenNonogram offers an optional one-time purchase (Remove Ads, com.zennonogram.puzzle.remove_ads) that unlocks unlimited instant Smart Hints with zero video ads.

If you reinstall the app or switch devices using the same Apple ID, open Remove Ads and tap Restore Purchases.
