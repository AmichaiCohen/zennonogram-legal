PRIVACY POLICY

ZenNonogram · Effective Date: 2026-11-25 · Developer: Amichai Cohen

Support & FAQ Privacy Policy

Overview

ZenNonogram respects your privacy and is designed to be played completely offline without creating an account.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

ZenNonogram does not operate first-party servers and does not collect, transmit, or store personal gameplay data externally. The following state is stored strictly locally on your device using on-device storage (localStorage / WebKit storage):

  • Current puzzle progress, grid size preference (5×5, 10×10, 15×15), solve statistics, and daily streak history.
  • App preferences including sound mute state and display language (English, Japanese, or Korean).
  • Your local In-App Purchase ("Remove Ads") entitlement flag and hint usage counters so ad-free hints work offline.

2. Optional Advertising (Google AdMob) & App Tracking Transparency

To support optional rewarded video hints in the free tier, ZenNonogram integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

  • If you tap Hint while online (and have not purchased Remove Ads), Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA if authorized via App Tracking Transparency), coarse IP-derived location, diagnostics, and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads.
  • Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt asks whether you allow tracking. Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" lets you continue playing ZenNonogram with non-personalized rewarded ads.
  • More details on Google's data practices: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Analytics & Remote Configuration (Google Firebase)

To understand gameplay balance and keep ZenNonogram reliable, we use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Remote Config:

  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous, aggregated usage and product interaction data (such as puzzle completions, hint usage, and app performance) not linked to your personal identity, used strictly to improve gameplay and app stability.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Fetches non-personal configuration values (such as feature thresholds and A/B test variants) without collecting personal data.
  • Firebase Privacy & Security policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

ZenNonogram offers one non-consumable purchase ("Remove Ads", product ID com.zennonogram.puzzle.remove_ads) processed directly by Apple via StoreKit. We never receive or store your credit card, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.

5. Children's Privacy

ZenNonogram does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All local ZenNonogram gameplay data remains on your device and can be permanently deleted at any time by uninstalling the app.

7. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Questions or requests may be submitted via github.com/AmichaiCohen/zennonogram-legal/issues.
